SkyOrb Replacement

ESP32 · left clock and right flight radar · ADS-B radar

Important: this page requires firmware binaries created with pio run and scripts/prepare_installer.py. The source package does not include precompiled binaries.

Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer, connect your 30-pin ESP32 by a data-capable USB cable, and choose Install. Installing may erase your existing SkyOrb configuration.

After installation, join SKYORB-Setup (password SKYORB1234), then visit 192.168.4.1. Rejoin your home Wi-Fi and open skyorb.local to configure your clock and radar.
