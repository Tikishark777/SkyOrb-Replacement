✈ SkyOrb Classic

Dual GC9A01 ESP32 displays · white/amber clock · green aircraft radar · original-style orange control panel.

Preview firmware, not yet tested on physical hardware. This installer works only after the GitHub firmware build and deployment have succeeded. Keep your original recovery installer bookmarked.

Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer with a USB data cable. Installing replaces your current firmware and might erase some saved settings.

After installation, connect to SKYORB-Setup (password SKYORB1234). Open http://192.168.4.1/setup, select your home Wi-Fi, and save. Then use http://192.168.4.1/ while still on SKYORB-Setup to set radar location. If your phone switches to home Wi-Fi, use the numeric IP shown on SkyOrb at startup.

Flight data from ADSB.lol and ADSB.fi; public feeds are subject to terms and availability. This is not an aviation safety device.
